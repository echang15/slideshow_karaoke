🎤 Slideshow Karaoke

Random slides. A ticking clock. Zero preparation allowed.

How to play

Inspired by the Toastmasters improv activity: each speaker gets a short speech (default 90 seconds), built from a sequence of random slides that auto-advance (default every 30 seconds). The speaker has to keep talking and weave each new slide into their speech as it appears — no script, no warning, no mercy.

Everyone in the group gets their own unique set of slides, so nobody can prepare by watching someone else go first.

Advanced: session code
